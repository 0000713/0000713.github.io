The criterion is the design. Identical corpus, identical starting characters — and the three rules disagree on the very first thing they do. Byte-pair encoding fuses u g because it is the commonest pair; WordPiece fuses g s instead, because although it occurs only five times, “s” never appears without “g”. Unigram fuses nothing at all.
