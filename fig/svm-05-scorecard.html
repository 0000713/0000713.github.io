EVALUATION / SIX QUESTIONS THAT ACTUALLY TEST MULTILINGUAL SUPPORT
ASK THESE INSTEAD
“Supports 22 languages” is a product claim, not a measurement. Every question below is answerable with a tokenizer file and an afternoon.
THE QUESTION TO STOP ASKING
“Does it support Hindi?”
1
Fertility
Tokens per word on representative native-script and romanised corpora — not on a translated benchmark.
MEASURE IT YOURSELF · IT TAKES MINUTES
2
Compression
Bytes or characters per token. Catches the mid-codepoint splitting that fertility alone hides.
3
Effective context
How much actual language fits in the advertised window — after system prompt, tools and output.
4
Serving impact
Prefill latency, KV-cache footprint and throughput on matched-content prompts, not matched token counts.
5
Economics
Cost per word, conversation, or completed task. Per-token pricing quietly bills fragmentation to the user.
THE ONE THAT HITS YOUR INVOICE
6
Quality
Does lower fertility preserve accuracy and fluency? Fewer tokens is not automatically better learning.
THE ASYMMETRY WORTH REMEMBERING
A model can accept your language, answer fluently, and still charge you 5× more per sentence than it charges an English speaker — with an identical parameter count and an identical advertised context window. That gap is decided before training begins.
